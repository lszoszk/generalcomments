lawyer who appeared for the author before the Court of Appeal had no retainer to
act, nor 41d he seek to obtain the author's express consent to appear before the
Court of Appeal and state that there were no grounds of appeal. In these

the services of a different attorney. It is submitted that an individual's right
to legal representation of his own choosing does not only comprise the trial but
